# StadiumBattleFX 2.1.0 is out!

Stadium 2 models and the trainer-portrait fix are now combined in one release.

**Download:** [StadiumBattleFX 2.1.0](https://github.com/anxiousintrovert/StadiumBattleFX/releases/tag/v2.1.0)

**What's new**
- Stadium 2 normal and shiny appearances for all 151 Gen 1 Pokemon
- Stadium 1 battle animations, timing, reactions, and move attachments retained
- Optional full-color Stadium trainer portraits during battle openings
- Fixes for Charizard, Wartortle, Blastoise, the full Pidgey line, Ponyta,
  Rapidash, and Moltres
- Blastoise's cannons restored and mounted on the upper shell
- Complete Ponyta/Rapidash/Moltres flame surfaces and preserved Gastly/Koffing/
  Weezing effects

**Install / update**
1. Download `STADIUM_BATTLE_FX-2.1.0.zip` from the GitHub release.
2. Install it with Gen1Recomp's mod manager and enable StadiumBattleFX.
3. Remove or disable the old standalone Stadium 2 Importer—it is built in now.
4. Open Options → **STADIUM ROM → IMPORT** and choose Pokemon Stadium (US) 1.0.
5. Open Options → **STADIUM 2 ROM → IMPORT** and choose Pokemon Stadium 2 (US).
6. Let both private caches finish building, then start a battle.

The public ZIP is ROM-free. Supply your own legally obtained dumps. `.z64`,
`.n64`, and `.v64` byte orders are accepted, but they must normalize to these
MD5 hashes:

`Pokemon Stadium (US) 1.0 — ed1378bc12115f71209a77844965ba50`

`Pokemon Stadium 2 (US) — 1561c75d11cedf356a8ddb1a4a5f9d5d`

Other regions or revisions will not import.
