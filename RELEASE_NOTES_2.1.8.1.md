# StadiumBattleFX 2.1.8.1

This compatibility hotfix fixes trainer-battle card ownership with Dramaless
Shape 2.0.x. When Dramaless temporarily uses its standalone staged renderer,
StadiumBattleFX now leaves the native trainer and Pokemon cards intact until
Dramaless has captured them.

The announcer cache is unrelated to this issue and does not need rebuilding.
